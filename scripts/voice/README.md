# Voice lines

> **2026-10-30:** the voices are made on this Mac with a local model (no ElevenLabs, no cost). The coach of the Learn episodes is a designed warm alto teacher, and the buddies speak their own working in three designed child voices (kid-a a calm boy, kid-b a girl, kid-c a brighter boy; `BUDDY_VOICE` in voice.ts says which buddy uses which). The typing lessons' coach lines are still the older ElevenLabs takes (Matilda) until they are remade: `node scripts/voice/generate.mjs --match '^(coach|lesson|finger|region)\.' --force` would remake them in the local coach voice. The Keypals' own old lines (dino.hello...) are not played.

**Writing a line:** read the guide at the top of `src/lib/content/voice.ts` first. Every Learn line has the words shown (`text`, digits and signs are fine) and the words said (`said`: whole sentences, numbers and signs as words, no colons). A test checks the spoken forms.

**Making the clips:** add the line to `src/lib/content/voice.ts` and run `node scripts/voice/generate.mjs`. It makes every clip that is missing or whose words or voice changed (`scripts/voice/made.json` remembers what each clip was made from), into `public/voice/`, and rewrites `src/lib/content/voice-manifest.json`. `--learn` also makes every Learn line not yet made locally; `--only id1,id2` remakes chosen lines; `--match <pattern>` limits to ids matching a pattern; `--dry-run` shows what it would make. Every clip is trimmed, faded at both ends, and levelled to about -20 LUFS (true peak under -1.5 dBTP).

**The local model:** `scripts/voice/local_tts.py` (Kokoro and OmniVoice, in a venv with the models under `~/.cache/keypals-voice`, about 1.7 GB; its header says how to set it up again). The designed voices are cloned from the reference takes in `scripts/voice/refs/`, so each voice stays the same across hundreds of lines; the voice table at the top of local_tts.py lists the others to try (`python3 scripts/voice/local_tts.py --list`). About 7 seconds a line on the Mac Mini. If a take comes out wrong, give the line a new `seed` (or respelled `text`) in `line-overrides.json` and remake it with `--only`.

**ElevenLabs** is still there, off by default: `node scripts/voice/generate.mjs --engine elevenlabs --only <id>` (key in `~/.keys/elevenlabs.key`; it stops if the account has under 20,000 credits left; `voice_id` and `settings` in `voices.json`).

**Checking:** `node scripts/voice/check.mjs` (or `--match '^(ep|bv)\.'` for the Learn lines) measures levels and transcribes each clip with the local Whisper (mlx_whisper) to compare it with the words it should say.
